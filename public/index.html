The Open Witness Archive

An original-fiction archive of memory, revision, and the lives a correction can change.

An evacuation bell rang in Leth. People remembered the sound and the lives it saved, but no one had rung it. Ivo Ren, a municipal corrections clerk, found the missing cause and put it back. The bell rang again. A child was safe, and the child's mother no longer remembered the person who had rescued him.

That was Ivo's first lesson in revision: a better outcome can take something real away. Years later, people called him Sovereign because even a final account of what he was could sometimes be revised. He still carries a brass frame from the corrections office, still worries about a cup in his mother's kitchen, and still cannot prove that the one remembering his childhood is the one who lived it.

This archive keeps the stories, records, contradictions, and refusals that a list of powers would miss.

How to read a claim

The archive separates canon events, in-universe theories, and scaling interpretations. A witness can be mistaken. An axiom can be disputed. An impressive interpretation does not become an event just because it sounds final. Late-story pages discuss the ferryman, the Closed King, and the apparent Author and therefore contain major revelations.

"A correction is an event. I forgot that when I was young." — Ivo Ren
