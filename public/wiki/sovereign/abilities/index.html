'''Sovereign's abilities''' are applications of Self-Refinement in particular accounts. They are grouped by what changes and what the change costs. The labels below describe demonstrated techniques, not a catalogue of universal immunities.

The measure

Ivo compares two descriptions using the measure, a brass registration frame from his human work. It can reveal a gap between accounts without identifying which is true. He discovered the evacuation bell's missing cause through a similar comparison. The tool is a focus and a reminder of his old vocation; it is not the source of the power.

Readdressing

An effect needs a target relation in its account. Ivo can revise how a subsequent state of himself participates in that relation when the relation leaves accessible residue. The tribunal's Absolute Attack executed against the defendant it had indexed. The later Ivo was no longer indexed that way. The episode leaves personal continuity unresolved. An effect that does not rely on such an address may offer no equivalent route.

Witness transfer

When one account explicitly interprets another, Ivo can carry a registered difference across the relation and propose a revision there. An omitted event in the fable of Leth became testimony in Leth. This requires a relation; it does not let him command an arbitrary external narrative.

The Deferred Ending

Ivo can hold a declared conclusion open while he examines its premises. A sentence of death, a final diagnosis, or the closing of an account may remain unresolved for the duration of a witnessable inquiry. Completed harm is not erased by the delay. He can still arrive too late.

The Unwritten State

Ivo may withdraw a bounded part of his self-description while its consequences remain. A pursuer once found warmth on a chair and a conversation still oriented toward its absent participant. The missing part cannot be directly reported by those within the affected account. The greater the omission, the harder it is for anyone to ratify a subsequent Ivo. See The Unwritten State.

Margin Concordance

Two incompatible accounts can be aligned so that each retains its own witness. This is how Ivo preserved contradictory reports of a rescue without forcing one to be false or treating every claim as true. It is a logical and narrative edit with a moral purpose: no witness becomes merely the error in someone else's record. It can leave action undecidable when the accounts require opposing remedies.

Counterfactual Mercy

Ivo can show a harmed person more than one possible repair and allow refusal. It is a procedure he adopted after nearly changing Sana's memories under an account that treated silence as consent. The person may choose a life Ivo believes worse. The ability does not tell him whether a reconstructed person is the original or whether consent survives radical identity change.

The Last Word

Ivo can ask an apparently unconditional effect to state its actual resolution conditions. The result may expose a mismatch between an absolute claim and the target relation through which it operates. This is an offensive semantic technique when the mismatch can be acted upon. It does not defeat a truthful, fully scoped claim by wordplay alone.

Passive and defensive pattern

Ivo tends to notice unexplained differences and can often revise before a local effect fully resolves. This is conditional. Awareness, time, and a surviving witness may be absent. A pre-causal attack, information erasure, or total closure is dangerous precisely because it can remove the route by which refinement would begin. Later witnesses may still detect a boundary of absence, but the story never assumes they always will.

Costs and limits

Every self-edit can weaken evidence that the new Ivo is the same subject. A successful defense may destroy a friendship, change another person's history, or leave a copy where a restoration was intended. His vow protects The Anchor Memory from intentional editing even when a hostile witness uses it against him. His knowledge and moral judgment remain fallible.
