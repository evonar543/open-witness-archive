Sovereign/Feats

'''Sovereign's feats''' are archived events with a stated mechanism, implication, and limit. A surviving account may be disputed; an implication is narrower than a claim of universal ability. See Sovereign/History for sequence and Sovereign/Axioms for the archivists' theory.

1. The floodgate chain — physical

Event: Human Ivo held a jammed floodgate chain while neighbors crossed a footbridge. Mechanism: Leverage, pain, and the help of Teren Oss. Implication: He risked himself before he could edit anything. Limit: This is no evidence of supernatural strength or invulnerability.

2. The unasked question — temporal

Event: He read a question on a survey proof six seconds before he asked it. Mechanism: He perceived a local description preceding its event. Implication: Leth's account could be read in advance. Limit: The event does not show universal control of time or certainty about every future.

3. The bell's cause — narrative

Event: Ivo restored a cause to an evacuation bell already remembered as having rung. Mechanism: Local account editing. Implication: A missing cause can be changed while its consequences have witnesses. Limit: The corrected history removed a mother's gratitude; repair did not preserve every relationship.

4. The abandoned sky — cosmic

Event: A flood was diverted through geography no previous Leth map allowed. Mechanism: Reality editing under Leth's account. Implication: Ivo could alter large-scale local world constraints. Limit: Teren ceased to remember their friendship, and the event grants no authority over unrelated worlds.

5. The lethal order — identity

Event: An order declared Ivo unable to perceive the description required to escape it; he changed his own perceptual capacity. Mechanism: Self-editing from a retained witness mismatch. Implication: Some foundational self-properties can be revised. Limit: His schoolbook then ceased to match his handwriting; the feat cannot prove an unbroken subject survived.

6. A home without ownership — conceptual

Event: Refugees excluded by a property rule gained standing to call their shelter home. Mechanism: Ivo revised the local concept of home while residents testified to its use. Implication: A category can change practical rights in an account. Limit: The change did not settle every property dispute or make Ivo morally correct.

7. Two rescue reports — logical

Event: Two incompatible reports of one rescue remained usable without making every statement true. Mechanism: Ivo changed the account's admissible inferences so each report retained its witness. Implication: Local rules of inference can be altered. Limit: This does not make contradiction a source of arbitrary power.

8. The unrecorded child — ontological

Event: A census that had excluded an unnamed child began to count her as existent. Mechanism: Ivo changed the register's criterion of existence. Implication: Ontological status can depend on account rules. Limit: He did not create an original child, and other accounts may still exclude her.

9. The Absolute Attack — semantic

Event: The tribunal's unavoidable, irreversible attack destroyed its indexed defendant; a later Ivo spoke from the gallery. Mechanism: Readdressing the relation that identified a continuation as the defendant. Implication: An effect can execute perfectly yet fail to include a revised continuation. Limit: The defendant died. The record does not prove the later voice belongs to the same consciousness, nor that every attack can be readdressed.

10. The fable of Leth — recursive

Event: An omission in an account reading Leth as a fable became testimony within Leth. Mechanism: Witness transfer across an explicit interpretive relation. Implication: Ivo can edit some relations among accounts. Limit: It does not establish command over every account or an infinite hierarchy.

11. The Author Event — anti-author

Event: An apparent author claimed every rebellion had been written; Ivo asked who had authored the certainty of that claim. Mechanism: He exposed a premise in the interpretation of authorship. Implication: The claim is contestable within the story. Limit: No version proves Ivo escaped authorship or controls a real writer.

12. The impossible arrival — paradox

Event: A city both recorded and denied Ivo's arrival, yet he could speak to witnesses of both reports. Mechanism: Margin Concordance preserved distinct witness relations. Implication: Contradictory accounts can be navigated without collapsing into one. Limit: A contradiction alone does not grant any desired conclusion.

13. The rival's response — adaptation

Event: A rival adapted to Ivo's prior revision; Ivo used the rival's response as the next encounter. Mechanism: The new response left residue. Implication: Self-Refinement can iterate. Limit: The record ends before a final result; it does not establish priority over every adaptive entity.

14. The archive's tier — comparison

Event: An in-world ranking archive had to display its selection rule beside its tier for Ivo. Mechanism: Ivo edited the relation from evidence to interpretation in that archive. Implication: A comparative model depends on what it selects. Limit: It does not invalidate all rankings or control real readers.

15. The untouched cup — emotional

Event: Ivo rejected an offer to settle the color and origin of his anchor by changing the past. Mechanism: Voluntary restraint under pressure. Implication: His vow can govern his use of power. Limit: Refusal does not prove the memory happened, or that leaving it alone was best for others.

16. The warm chair — unwritten

Event: A pursuer found Ivo's warmth on a chair and a conversation shaped around an absent participant. Mechanism: A bounded Unwritten State. Implication: Consequences can remain when a self-description is withheld. Limit: Withdrawal threatens the witness needed for Ivo's continuation; universal survival is unestablished.

17. The unfinished mercy — consent

Event: The Last Mercy could complete every account in the archive as one painless life. Ivo's ordinary escape methods failed. He made room for Sana to answer its offer without either Ivo or the presence choosing for her; she refused a future in which she could never answer again. The presence withdrew its completion. Mechanism: Margin Concordance held two accounts legible, and the Unowned Answer changed Ivo's own relation to another person's consent. The presence's claim of mercy then had to face an answer it had not authored. Implication: Ivo can defeat a totalizing plan from within its reach when the plan depends on claiming the good of people it would overrule. Limit: The presence retained its power and chose not to betray its purpose. An indifferent totality could have erased the witness. The flood and earlier losses remained.

18. The white interval — prior answer

Event: An indifferent force erased the first Ivo, the office, its witnesses, and the accounts to which he might have fled, but did not complete its closure of Leth. A successor appeared at the resolution of the act and interrupted its continuation. Mechanism: Before closure, Ivo formed a conditional self-revision that would resolve with the act rather than wait for later residue. Implication: Loss of every witness after an attack does not establish that no prepared revision can occur during it. Limit: The first Ivo ended, several people did not return, and no one can prove the successor had his earlier consciousness. A force that prevents preparation or controls the revision's resolution poses a different challenge. See The White Interval.

19. The court of endings — self-jurisdiction

Event: Final authorities from incompatible accounts assembled a court to assign Sovereign a last description. At the court's hearing, the Unruled Witness let each keep its own authority but ended their joint claim to define him. A universal defeat of the opposition was available as his first verdict; he declined it. Mechanism: Self-jurisdiction denied imported definitions automatic authority over him, and the peak selected a consequence without dismantling the opposing worlds. Implication: His unrestrained state is not dependent on the clerk's witnessed revision procedure or a higher rung in the court's hierarchy. Limit: The court is an event in Sovereign's fiction; it does not bind real authors or adjudicate every independently written crossover.

Failures and anti-feats

The bell correction removed gratitude. The flood diversion cost Teren's friendship. Ivo reconstructed a drowned ferryman whose memories were complete but who could not recognize his husband. He nearly changed Sana's memories under a false rule of implicit consent; she left. An alternate Ivo erased grief from a city by erasing its dead from kinship. These failures matter because stronger editing would not by itself answer whether the new result was just or continuous with what preceded it.

Interpretation guide

Canon event identifies what a surviving story presents. In-universe theory explains the event through Sovereign/Self-Refinement. Scaling interpretation compares versions and encounter rules. The first eighteen events mostly record the bounded life Ivo chose. The nineteenth records a peak-state choice. None is a complete inventory of the Unruled Witness; its design claim and the events used to explain it should be read together.
