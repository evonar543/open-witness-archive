Sovereign/Quotes

Selected quotations are drawn from surviving accounts of Ivo Ren. Attribution is given by scene where known; some lines are disputed because revisions changed their witnesses.

To ordinary people

"We can sit until the rain stops. I am poor at stopping rain without changing where it falls." — at a flooded shelter
"Eat first," Teren told him. "You can be unbearable on an empty stomach." Ivo later repeated it to a child preparing to confront a god. — Teren's river notes

To rulers and enemies

"You may own every answer in this room. I am asking who locked the door." — to a cosmic tyrant
"Within this account, there is nothing you cannot do." — to The Closed King

On descriptions

"I thought that was the sky. It seems to be a sentence about one." — on discovering a second account
"A correction is an event. I forgot that when I was young." — after the evacuation bell

On identity

"Mira laughed at the cup. Or I have told myself so often that the laugh learned to sound like hers." — on The Anchor Memory
"Tell me what you chose when no one would know." — to another Ivo

On power

"Then you have missed several people I could not save." — when called omnipotent
"Ask someone whose life I changed without permission. You may get a better answer." — when asked whether he is God
"A good reason to leave a thing broken. I have lost to those before." — when asked what could defeat him

The Author Event

"Every rebellion you ever performed was something I wrote." — the apparent Author
"Then who wrote your certainty?" — Ivo Ren

The Author Event has incompatible surviving versions. The lines are common to them, but the exchange's outcome is not settled. See Sovereign/History.
