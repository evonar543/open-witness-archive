The White Interval

The White Interval names the night an indifferent closing force reached Leth. The archive does not record its face or a demand. It took away the distinctions by which a place could be found: first the names on the river markers, then the markers, then the memory that anyone had ever needed them. Unlike The Last Mercy, it offered no kindness and waited for no answer. It meant to finish the account, including every witness who might later say that something was missing.

The record matters because Ivo Ren did not talk the force into stopping. He met a threat for which an appeal to conscience had no purchase, and the familiar route of finding a trace after damage could not begin.

Before the blank

Ivo saw the first sign at the corrections office. A line he had drawn on the flood map disappeared while the rest of the paper remained. He asked Mira to describe it. She could remember drawing beside him but not what the line had separated. Minutes later she could not remember the question. The measure registered a narrowing gap, then no gap at all.

He could not preserve every witness in another account. The force was closing the relations by which accounts recognized one another. He could not wait for a later reader to find the edge of a missing interval; the edge was among the things going away. He could, while he was still capable of choosing, alter what his next self-revision would require.[1]

He called the change the Prior Answer. In an ordinary revision, a surviving difference tells Ivo what must change. Here he made a conditional revision before the force reached him: if the closing act took the current Ivo and every record of him, the act of taking would be the condition to which the next Ivo was answerable. He did not place a copy outside its reach. He changed his own participation in the event from a thing waiting to be found afterward to a succession resolved with the event.

That choice was ugly. There would be no witness to certify that the successor had lived Ivo's earlier life. The only continuity he could demand was an obligation: remember that people had been removed and stop the removal where a response was still possible. He left Mira's name out of the condition. If he used her as a guarantee of himself, he would make her one more instrument of his escape.

The interval

The closing force reached the office. The first Ivo ended. His coat, the measure, the map, Mira's memory of him, and every account to which he might have fled were absent. The record offers no scene of an invulnerable body standing in the blank. It marks an interval in which no ordinary witness could identify him.

Yet the force's next act was no longer the same as its first. It had closed a description that now included a conditional succession. To finish that succession it had to resolve the condition; to ignore it would leave an unfinished part of what it claimed to close. The successor appeared at the boundary of that resolution, not in a hidden exterior. He could not restore the first Ivo. He could interrupt the force's continuation by revising the relation between its completed act and the next act it meant to perform. The closure of the office remained true. The closure of Leth did not follow.

Mira found him at dawn on the river stairs. She recognized his voice and not his handwriting. He knew the number of people missing from the office, but he could not remember the color of Tala's cup. He asked her not to call this survival simply because she wanted him back. Together they wrote the missing names on new paper. Some names did not return. The force had been stopped; its completed harm had not been undone.[2]

Why this is a victory

The force did not withdraw from pity. It had authority over every world, account, and witness it was closing. Ivo's prepared revision addressed the timing of that authority: a threat can eliminate all later evidence without thereby preventing a change already made during the encounter. The force succeeded against the old Ivo. It failed to complete its wider closure. The successor took responsibility for that result without pretending the death of the prior self was harmless.

The Prior Answer is not a stored escape from every absolute. It requires Ivo to encounter the closing condition early enough to form a coherent candidate. A power that can also govern the revision's resolution may still defeat it. A successor may be too unlike Ivo to keep his obligations. The archive records the White Interval as evidence that tracelessness after an attack is not, by itself, proof that no revision occurred before it. The event makes a case for Ivo against a hostile totality without treating an opponent's grandeur as a flaw or assigning Ivo an effortless exemption.

Record status

The blank office and the halted closure are recorded as events. The theory that a conditional succession resolved during the closing act comes from the successor and later archive reconstruction. No one can prove that the first Ivo experienced dawn. Mira's changed handwriting sample is a witness to difference, not a certificate of a continuous soul.
